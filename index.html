About the Channel

Daily Recitation TV HD was created to make the Holy Quran freely available to anyone, anywhere — helping people listen to, learn, and live with the Book of Allah through beautiful, high-quality recitations.

This channel runs no ads and accepts no collaborations, partnerships, or sponsorships. It is a sincere effort, purely for the sake of Allah ﷻ. If the recitations benefit you, the best support is your duʿā — and sharing them with others.
